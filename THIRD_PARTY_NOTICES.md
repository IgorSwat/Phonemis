## Data

### English lexicons

- **Files:** `data/en-us/lexicon_full.json`, `data/en-us/lexicon_small.json`, `data/en-gb/lexicon_full.json`,
  `data/en-gb/lexicon_small.json`
- **Source:** the gold and silver pronunciation dictionaries of [misaki](https://github.com/hexgrad/misaki)
  by hexgrad.
- **License:** Apache License 2.0, see [`licenses/misaki-Apache-2.0.txt`](licenses/misaki-Apache-2.0.txt).
- **Changes:** the dictionaries were modified for Phonemis:
  - flattened, so that a word's part-of-speech variants become separate `word|TAG` entries
    ([`scripts/tools/create_lexicon.py`](scripts/tools/create_lexicon.py));
  - the gold and silver dictionaries were merged into one file
    ([`scripts/tools/concatenate_lexicons.py`](scripts/tools/concatenate_lexicons.py));
  - the small lexicons are reduced versions of the full ones.

### English part-of-speech tagger

- **Files:** `data/en-us/tagger.json`, `data/en-gb/tagger.json` (and the tag descriptions in `tags.json`)
- **Source:** HMM probabilities computed by [`scripts/tools/create_tagger.py`](scripts/tools/create_tagger.py) from
  the sentences of the [Brown Corpus](http://www.hit.uib.no/icame/brown/bcm.html) (W. N. Francis and H. Kučera, as
  distributed with NLTK), tagged with [spaCy](https://spacy.io).
- **License:** NLTK lists the Brown Corpus as *"May be used for non-commercial purposes."* Treat these files as
  **not cleared for commercial use**.
- **Commercial use:** the tagger is optional. Leave out `Config::tagger`, and English is phonemized without it,
  with weaker handling of words whose pronunciation depends on their part of speech (*read*, *used to*). A tagger
  trained on commercially usable text is planned.

### Neural phonemizer models

- **Files:** `data/*/phonemizer_*.bin`
- **Source:** Protophone models trained by the Phonemis authors on pronunciations generated with
  [misaki](https://github.com/hexgrad/misaki): its gold and silver dictionaries for English and, for the other
  languages, its espeak-ng backend ([espeak-ng](https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng) is licensed under GPL-3.0).
- **License:** MIT, as the rest of Phonemis. The models contain only weights learned from those pronunciations: no
  code or dictionaries of misaki or espeak-ng.
